Web app (PWA)

Add to your home screen for a full-screen app with offline play.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open kinkeda.com/app in Safari (other browsers on iOS cannot install PWAs).
  2. Tap the Share button (□↑) at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add, then open Kinkeda from your home screen.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open kinkeda.com/app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the Install banner if it appears at the bottom of the game, or
  3. Tap the menu (⋮) → Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm, then launch Kinkeda from your home screen or app drawer.

The web version updates automatically when you revisit — no manual reinstall needed.
