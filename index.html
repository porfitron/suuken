Quick rounds

Guess the next number with all players' thumbs - your turn is your chance to score. First to be right twice wins the match.

Play anywhere

iPhone, Android, or web. Install the app or open the browser version — works offline once installed.

How it works

  1. The Setup: Two players face each other, each controlling one side of the screen. Each player starts with two thumbs in play.
  2. The Action: Both players simultaneously decide how many thumbs to press onto the screen (0, 1, or 2).
  3. The Guess: Players take turns guessing the total number of thumbs that will be pressed down by both players combined.
  4. Winning a Round: If you correctly guess the total on your turn, you win the round and get to "hide" one of your thumbs.
  5. Winning the Match: The first player to win two rounds (and put both thumbs away) wins the match!
