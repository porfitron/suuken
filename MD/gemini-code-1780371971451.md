# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Suuken PWA

## Overview
Suuken is a 2-player Progressive Web App (PWA) game played on a single multi-touch screen (ideal for tablets/phones placed flat on a table). It adapts the traditional Japanese rock-paper-scissors-style game "Suuken" where players guess the total number of raised thumbs. Here, the four corners of the screen represent the players' thumbs.[cite: 4]

## Mechanics & Rules
1. **Setup:** 
   - Player 1 (P1) is positioned at the bottom of the screen. Their "hands" are the Bottom-Left and Bottom-Right corners.[cite: 4]
   - Player 2 (P2) is positioned at the top of the screen (UI flipped 180° for them). Their "hands" are the Top-Left and Top-Right corners.[cite: 4]
   - Initially, all four corners are active.[cite: 4]
2. **Turn Sequence:**
   - **Guess Phase:** The active player selects a number from 0 to X (where X is the total number of active corners left in the game).[cite: 4]
   - **Countdown Phase:** A 3-2-1 countdown begins.[cite: 4]
   - **Action Phase:** During "GO!", both players simultaneously tap and hold the active corners they wish to "raise". (They can raise 0, 1, or 2 corners, assuming their corners haven't been eliminated).[cite: 4]
   - **Resolution Phase:** After a 1-second hold, the game tallies the pressed corners. If the total matches the active player's guess, the active player successfully "removes" one of their hands (one of their corners becomes inactive/dark).[cite: 4]
3. **Win Condition:** The first player to eliminate both of their corners wins.[cite: 4]
4. **Turn Rotation:** The active player role switches after every resolution phase.[cite: 4]

## Key Features
- **Multi-Touch Support:** Must accurately register up to 4 simultaneous touches.[cite: 4]
- **PWA Capabilities:** Installable to the home screen, offline capable, and full-screen (to prevent browser UI from interfering with gameplay).[cite: 4]
- **Responsive Layout:** 100vh / 100vw strict bounds. Prevent scrolling or zooming.[cite: 4]
- **Visual Feedback:** Corners light up (hot) when touched. The center of the screen acts as the game dashboard.[cite: 4]

## Target Devices
- iPads, Android Tablets, and modern smartphones supporting 4+ point multi-touch.[cite: 4]
